Depth comparison

Model: Depth Anything V2 Small predicts relative inverse depth for each part of the image: higher values suggest nearer surfaces. For display, the prediction is scaled from black (far) to white (near) using this image’s minimum and maximum. Values are not meters and cannot be compared across photos.

Drag the slider. Light = near; dark = far.

3D preview

Construction: The relative-depth map and photo colors are sampled onto a grid, then adjacent points are connected into a surface. The preview assumes a 60° camera field of view and assigns arbitrary depth values; it is not a measured 3D reconstruction. Gaps and stretched edges are artifacts, and hidden surfaces are missing.

Drag the image to rotate it.

Estimated distance

Model: A separate Depth Anything V2 Small metric model predicts camera-axis depth in meters. Choose the indoor or outdoor checkpoint before uploading, then click the photo to read the estimate at that point. The browser resizes the image to 518 × 518 pixels for the metric model and does not use camera metadata or calibration.

Click a point in the photo, or use the arrow keys.
